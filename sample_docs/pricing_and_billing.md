# Nimbus Cloud: Pricing and Billing

Nimbus Cloud offers three pricing tiers. All prices are listed in US dollars
and billed either monthly or annually.

## Plans

- **Starter** — $0 per month. Includes 10 GB of Nimbus Storage, 20 compute
  hours per month, and community support only. Intended for evaluation and
  personal projects, not production workloads.
- **Team** — $49 per user per month. Includes 500 GB of pooled Nimbus
  Storage, unlimited compute hours (subject to fair-use limits), up to 3
  Nimbus Pipelines, and standard email support with a 24-hour response SLA.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing, negotiated with the sales team. Includes
  unlimited storage, unlimited pipelines, a dedicated customer success
  manager, single sign-on (SSO), and premium support with a 1-hour response
  SLA for severity-1 incidents.

## Annual Billing Discount

Customers who commit to annual billing instead of monthly billing receive a
**15% discount** on the Team and Enterprise plans. The discount is applied
automatically at checkout when the annual billing option is selected and
does not require a separate coupon code.

## Overage Charges

Storage usage beyond a plan's included quota is billed at **$0.08 per GB**
per month on the Team plan. Compute usage on the Team plan beyond fair-use
limits is billed at $0.00042 per compute-second. Enterprise overage rates
are defined in the customer's contract.

## Payment Methods and Invoicing

Team plan customers are billed automatically via credit card each billing
cycle. Enterprise customers may pay via invoice with net-30 terms. All plans
can be canceled at any time; downgrades take effect at the start of the next
billing cycle, and Nimbus Cloud does not offer prorated refunds for partial
months.

## Free Trial

New Team-plan signups receive a 14-day free trial with full Team-tier
limits before the first charge is applied. No credit card is required to
start a Starter-tier account, but a credit card is required to start a
Team-tier trial.
