# Nimbus Cloud: Onboarding and Support

## Getting Started

New Starter and Team accounts are self-serve: sign up on the website,
verify your email, and create your first Nimbus Storage bucket or Nimbus
Compute job from the in-app quickstart wizard. Most new users complete
their first successful job run within 20 minutes of signing up, according
to internal onboarding analytics.

Enterprise customers are assigned a dedicated **Customer Success Manager
(CSM)** who runs a structured onboarding program: a kickoff call within the
first week of contract signature, a technical architecture review within
the first 30 days, and a quarterly business review (QBR) thereafter.

## Support Tiers

- **Community** (Starter plan) — access to the public community forum and
  documentation site. No guaranteed response time.
- **Standard** (Team plan) — email support with a **24-hour response SLA**
  during business days.
- **Premium** (Enterprise plan) — a dedicated shared Slack channel with the
  Nimbus support team, phone escalation, and a **1-hour response SLA** for
  severity-1 incidents, 4-hour SLA for severity-2, and next-business-day
  for severity-3.

## Training and Documentation

All customers have access to the public documentation site and a library of
video tutorials. Enterprise customers additionally receive up to two
custom, private training sessions per year for their engineering team,
scheduled through their CSM.

## Churn and Cancellation

Customers who wish to cancel can do so from the billing settings page at
any time. The support team conducts an offboarding call for any Enterprise
customer who cancels, both to gather feedback and to ensure a clean data
export before the account is deactivated. Nimbus Cloud retains customer
data for 30 days after cancellation before permanent deletion, in case the
customer wishes to reactivate.
